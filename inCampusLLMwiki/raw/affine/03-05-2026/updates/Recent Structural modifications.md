# Recent Structural modifications

* 01-05-2026 - Consent-Based Student Insight Access was introduced as a controlled structural extension for future campus-level collaboration. Since other **campus sectors may collect information about students’ non-academic interests**, InCampus can support this direction by reusing existing profile, activity, and participation data. However, this access is not treated as a default admin capability: identifiable student interests and activity history may be exposed to authorized campus staff **only when the student has explicitly consented**. This adds a new consent attribute to the Student Account entity and requires future admin insight views to enforce campus scope, consent checks, and least-privilege access.
* 26-04-2026 - **Sign-Up Credential Alignment**: the sign-up flow now includes both university email verification and password creation. The verified university email confirms academic eligibility, while the password is stored as a secure hash and used for future email-and-password login. This adds `PasswordHash` to the Student Account entity.
* 22-04-2026 - **Receive Activity Reminder** is now included in the MVP as a notification branch in the **Notifications and System Flow** subgroup. The flow sends a reminder only for students still joined in a scheduled upcoming activity; if the student has already left or the activity has been cancelled, the reminder is suppressed or superseded by the cancellation flow. This change was reflected in the NSF subgroup DFD and in the CRUD matrix.
* 19-04-2026 - **Set Activity Date and Time** is no longer treated as a standalone Hosting and Lifecycle use case. Date and time selection and validation are now modeled as an internal part of **Create Activity**. The Hosting and Lifecycle analysis should therefore treat activity initialization as one single creation flow, not as two separate interactions. No additional data store is introduced by this structural change.
